/*!
 * liquid-glass.js — physically-derived Liquid Glass for the web.
 *
 * Real refraction (not glassmorphism): builds a rounded-rect SDF, derives a
 * surface-normal displacement vector field from a convex-squircle bezel
 * profile, rasterises it into an R/G displacement map, and feeds it to
 * <feDisplacementMap> used as `backdrop-filter`.
 *
 * Chromium-only for the refraction tier. Safari/Firefox get a convincing
 * layered fallback (blur + tint + rim + specular). See SKILL.md.
 *
 * No dependencies. MIT.
